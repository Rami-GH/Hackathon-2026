# Business Model Canvas (BMC)
## Project: CyberTrust Nation (AmanNation)
**UAE Hackathon 2026 — Theme: Digital Trust & Cyber-Secure Nation**

---

### 1. Value Propositions
- **For Citizens & Residents**:
  - Instant, zero-friction verification of suspicious links, SMS notifications, and emails.
  - Protection against financial loss, identity theft, and deepfake impersonation scams.
  - Uncompromised trust and confidence when engaging with digital government services.
- **For Government Entities & Critical Infrastructure Operators**:
  - Sub-second autonomous incident mitigation (ransomware, 4.2 Tbps DDoS) ensuring 100% service continuity.
  - Zero citizen downtime during large-scale cyber incidents.
  - Automated compliance with the UAE National Cybersecurity Strategy 2025–2031.
  - Cryptographic governance framework for securing autonomous agentic AI deployments.

---

### 2. Customer Segments
- **B2C (Public Segment)**:
  - 10+ million UAE citizens, residents, and visitors utilizing digital government portals (TAMM, DubaiNow, ICP, UAE PASS).
- **B2G (Government Sector)**:
  - Federal and local government entities (TDRA, Dubai Digital Authority, Abu Dhabi Digital Authority).
  - Law enforcement & cyber agencies (UAE Cyber Security Council, Dubai eCrime, Abu Dhabi Police).
- **B2B (Critical Infrastructure & Enterprise)**:
  - Financial institutions, stock exchanges (ADX, DFM), Securities and Commodities Authority (SCA).
  - Energy & utility providers (DEWA, TAQA, ADNOC).
  - Logistics, transport, and telecom operators (e&, du, RTA, Emirates Post).

---

### 3. Channels
- **Direct Digital Touchpoints**:
  - Web & Mobile Progressive Web App (PWA) with instant scan capabilities.
  - UAE PASS integration: embedded trust-verification widget inside native government apps.
  - WhatsApp / SMS Automated Verification Bot connected to TDRA honeynet.
- **Institutional Integration**:
  - API Gateway connected to Federal FedNet infrastructure.
  - Live Bayanat.ae Open Data portal publishing public trust metrics.

---

### 4. Customer Relationships
- **Transparency & Trust**: Real-time national digital health dashboards showcasing service uptime and deflected threats.
- **Proactive Community Watch**: Rewarding citizen reporting through gamified cyber safety drills and alerts.
- **Enterprise SLA**: 99.999% availability guarantee with sub-second autonomous failover playbooks.

---

### 5. Revenue & Value Streams
- **National Economic Preservation**:
  - Deflecting estimated AED 450M+ in potential ransomware payouts, fraudulent card transactions, and critical infrastructure downtime.
- **B2B / SME Cyber Resilience SaaS**:
  - Tiered subscription for small-and-medium enterprises (SMEs) providing affordable, plug-and-play agentic protection without requiring in-house security teams.
- **Government Institutional Security Licensing**:
  - National infrastructure maintenance and sovereign cloud orchestration contract.

---

### 6. Key Resources
- **Proprietary AI Models**:
  - Fine-tuned NLP semantic phishing classifier and lookalike domain detection engine.
  - Multi-agent autonomous incident orchestration algorithms.
- **National Data Feeds**:
  - Bayanat.ae open datasets, TDRA domain whitelist registries, and UAE Cyber Security Council threat feeds.
- **Infrastructure**:
  - Distributed sovereign cloud microVM clusters and Anycast DDoS scrubbing centers.

---

### 7. Key Activities
- **Continuous AI Threat Modeling**: Monitoring emerging attack vectors (AI voice deepfakes, quishing, supply-chain exploits).
- **Autonomous Playbook Execution**: Real-time traffic scrubbing, token invalidation, and microVM failover.
- **Public Awareness Campaigns**: Running gamified cyber hygiene simulations and community awareness drives.
- **Data Governance & Publishing**: Maintaining quarterly open telemetry on Bayanat.ae.

---

### 8. Key Partners
- **Telecommunications and Digital Government Regulatory Authority (TDRA)**: Whitelist authority and telecom infrastructure coordination.
- **UAE Cyber Security Council (CSC)**: National incident response coordination and threat intelligence sharing.
- **UAE PASS**: Cryptographic biometric authentication backbone.
- **Major Telecom Operators (e& / du)**: Network-level SMS filter sync and DNS scrubbing.
- **Academic & Research Institutions**: Continuous validation and red-teaming of autonomous AI defense models.

---

### 9. Cost Structure
- **Cloud Infrastructure & Scrubbing**: High-bandwidth Anycast network capacity and ephemeral microVM nodes.
- **Model Training & Maintenance**: Continuous retraining on localized UAE phishing dialects and threat intelligence vectors.
- **Compliance & Auditing**: Regular third-party penetration testing and cryptographic enclave audits.
- **Community Outreach**: Public awareness educational materials and workshop events.
